# Enclosure (3D-printable)

3D-printable housing with a C-shaped interference-fit clamp for the prosthetic tube. It holds the battery and the circuit board; the coin motor and the FSR cable route externally.

**Files (STL/STEP) are being added.** Until then, see the prototype photos in `../docs/photos/` for the geometry.

Printing notes: standard FDM settings; the clamp relies on the print's elasticity for the interference fit on the prosthetic tube — orient the part so layer lines do not cross the clamp arms perpendicularly, and adjust the clamp inner diameter to the target tube (30 mm is the common modular standard).
